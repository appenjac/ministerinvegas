What You Need to Know

How to Get a Las Vegas Marriage License

Getting a Las Vegas marriage license is quick, simple, and one of the easiest processes in the world. You can typically obtain your Las Vegas marriage license in just minutes and get married the same day.

Online Pre-Application: Fill out your Las Vegas Marriage License Pre-Application online (Clark County Clerk), up to 60 days in advance. More details: How to get married in Las Vegas.

Las Vegas Marriage Bureau
Address: 201 Clark Ave., Las Vegas, Nevada 89101
Hours: 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight)
Open: 7 days a week, 365 days a year
Cost: $102.00

After Your Ceremony

Your marriage certificate will be filed by me as your Wedding Officiant within two days of your ceremony. Within five days you may go online to purchase a certified copy of the marriage certificate from the Clark County Clerk's Records Search & Order system.

Apostille Document

If you reside outside the U.S. and your country requires an apostille, then an apostille can be ordered from the Nevada Secretary of State's office in Carson City, Nevada. You will use the certified marriage certificate to send with the order form and credit card payment to their office. Important: The Secretary's office does not send return mail internationally.
